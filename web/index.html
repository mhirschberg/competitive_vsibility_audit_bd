Workshop audits use an anonymous session. For personal trials, Google sign-in supplies your name and email so we can keep your reports with your account. The organizer can see registered accounts and audit counts.

Account and audit records and report files are stored in Supabase’s Ireland region. Audit jobs run on Google Cloud in Belgium and use Bright Data. The website is delivered through Firebase’s global CDN. If you request a report-ready email, your address is sent to Resend, which stores email data in the US.
